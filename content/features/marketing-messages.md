## In 30 seconds

1. **Consent first.** No promotional email, SMS or DM without express or implied consent. [casl-consent]
2. **Identify yourself and let people leave.** Every commercial message needs sender info and a free, easy unsubscribe, honoured within 10 business days. [casl-unsubscribe]
3. **Don't mislead.** Subject lines, sender names, links and prices must be accurate, with all mandatory fees included. [competition-misleading-messages]

## Is my message "commercial"?

| Message | Commercial? | Consent needed? | Sender ID + unsubscribe? |
|---|---|---|---|
| Promo: "Earn 4% — open a savings account" | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| e-Transfer confirmation, no promo | Consent-exempt category (s.6(6)(b)) | No | Yes (s.6(2) still applies) |
| e-Transfer confirmation **+ a cross-sell banner** | **Yes, no longer "solely" transactional** | **Yes** | Yes |
| Fraud / security alert | Consent-exempt category (s.6(6)(c)) | No | Yes (s.6(2) still applies) |
| Message in a secure, logged-in banking inbox | Exempt (GIC Regs 3(e)) | No | No |
| Reply to a customer's support question | Exempt (GIC Regs 3(b)) | No | No |
| First refer-a-friend invite naming the referrer | Yes, exemption applies (GIC Regs 4) | No, first message only | Yes |
| Promotional push notification | Unsettled | Treat as yes | Treat as yes |

*Section numbers refer to CASL and the GIC Regulations. Open the rule pages above for the full citations and links to the official text.*

## Pre-build checklist

**Consent capture**
- [ ] Marketing opt-in checkbox is **unchecked** by default
- [ ] Opt-in is separate from Terms & Conditions acceptance
- [ ] Consent text names the company, purpose, contact info, and says consent can be withdrawn
- [ ] Consent is logged (timestamp, channel, exact wording, form/app version)
- [ ] Implied consent (existing customers) has an expiry tracked, or we collect express consent instead

**Every commercial message**
- [ ] Sender name, mailing address, and a phone/email/web contact are included (or linked)
- [ ] Unsubscribe is free, in the same channel, needs no login
- [ ] Unsubscribe link works for 60+ days after sending
- [ ] Unsubscribes are processed (including queued sends) within 10 business days

**Content**
- [ ] Subject line and sender name accurately reflect the content
- [ ] Headline offers show conditions nearby
- [ ] Prices include all mandatory fees

**Referrals**
- [ ] Invite names the referrer and says it's a referral
- [ ] No follow-ups to referred friends without their own consent

**Quebec customers** *(rules coming soon)*
- [ ] Law 25 consent for any personal info used to target or personalise
- [ ] Messages available in French

## Common mistakes

- Adding a promo banner to "transactional" emails, which makes them commercial.
- Treating "they're a customer" as permanent consent. Implied consent expires.
- Referral reminder sequences sent to friends who never opted in.
- "Re: your account" subject lines on promotional emails.
