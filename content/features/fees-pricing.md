## In 30 seconds

1. **Publish every deposit account fee, and give 30 days' notice of any new or higher fee.** [fcac-fee-disclosure]
2. **No add-on without a clear "yes":** customers must explicitly agree before you add a product or paid feature. Using it doesn't count as agreeing. [fcac-express-consent-products]
3. **No pressure tactics or forced bundles.** Bundle discounts are fine; making one product a condition of another isn't. [fcac-undue-pressure]
4. **Prices must be real:** include all mandatory fees in the price you advertise (no drip pricing). [competition-misleading-messages]

## Bank or not?

| You are… | Bank Act rules (FCAC) | Competition Act | Provincial consumer protection |
|---|---|---|---|
| Bank, authorized foreign bank, federal credit union | Yes | Yes | Generally no, for banking products |
| Provincial credit union | No. Provincial rules apply | Yes | Yes |
| Non-bank fintech (wallet, card program manager, lender) | No | Yes | Yes |

*Provincial consumer protection rules are coming soon. Until then, check with compliance for each province you serve.*

## Pre-build checklist

**Fee disclosure**
- [ ] Public fee schedule listing every account fee
- [ ] Fee page linked from sign-up and account settings
- [ ] Cheque hold periods disclosed

**Changing a fee**
- [ ] Notice at least 30 days before the effective date (allow extra time for mailed notices)
- [ ] Notice says where to get more information
- [ ] If there's no statement: 60 days of public notice, including ATMs for personal accounts

**Selling products and add-ons**
- [ ] Explicit opt-in action with price and key terms on the same screen
- [ ] Agreement or written confirmation sent right after consent
- [ ] No auto-enrolment into paid features
- [ ] Offers labelled as financial products, not just rewards

**Advertising and sales**
- [ ] Advertised prices include all mandatory fees
- [ ] Conditions ("with a $3,000 minimum balance") shown prominently
- [ ] No fake urgency, confirm-shaming, or required bundles

## Common mistakes

- Shipping a price change in a release that goes live the same day.
- Turning on paid overdraft protection by default.
- "$0 monthly fee*" with the asterisk hiding a mandatory charge.
- "Claim your reward" buttons that actually apply for credit.
